A little guidance

Keep it clear.
Give it room.

Leave at least one stem-width of clear space around the symbol. Use the symbol at 24 pixels or larger, and the horizontal wordmark at 140 pixels or larger.

Use blue or black on light backgrounds, and white on dark backgrounds. Keep the original proportions. Avoid shadows, outlines, gradients, rotation, or placing the mark on a busy image.

02 — A more thoughtful pathway

The next right step.
Then the next.

Our goal is to help clinicians narrow uncertainty with each decision, so people spend less time navigating care and more time moving forward.

03 — Open foundations

Small, open pieces.
A bigger possibility.

At the core is HiperHealth, an open-source computational library with a plugin-based approach to clinical workflows. Installable “skills” make it possible to extend the pipeline around different needs.

Hipercare’s service is planned as proprietary software, built by orchestrating reusable open-source components. Shared foundations. A focused experience.

A few things to know

Starting with clarity.

Is Hipercare already providing patient care?

No. Hipercare is an early-stage healthcare startup. This website presents the direction we want to develop with collaborators; it is not a patient service or a source of medical advice.

Is Hipercare open source?

The planned Hipercare service is proprietary. HiperHealth is an open-source library, and the broader approach is to build and share reusable open-source components.

Is there a company I can invest in today?

Hipercare is not yet a legally incorporated company. We welcome exploratory conversations with potential investors and sponsors. Any future arrangement would need its own agreed structure and terms.

Do I need to work in technology to help?

No. Clinical experience, research, knowledge of underserved communities, and support for the mission can all help shape better decisions about what to build.
